# Background: small things going on around people, drawn three a turn so nothing the player does is the only
# thing anyone could react to. One per line, "- [place, place] text" (tags optional); setup adds ones for the story's world.
# Options in the brackets: "sticky N" keeps it going N more turns, "cooldown N" rests it N turns after (default 6),
# "group G" lets only one line of a group go on at a time. Example: "- [sticky 4; group weather] Rain starts."

- A bell rings in the distance.

- Someone coughs on the other side of the wall.
- A door bangs somewhere in the neighbourhood.

- [sticky 3] A clock ticks loudly in the quiet.
- [sticky 1] Two people argue in the street over a price.
- A delivery is dropped off next door with a thud.
- A smell of cooking drifts in from a neighbour.
